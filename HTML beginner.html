Top 3 Anime Awards 2020-2026

2020

The 4th Crunchyroll Anime Awards in 2020 featured unique milestones,
unexpected co-hosts, and major wins for breakout series.

Award name winner Image
Anime of the Year Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba
Best Animation Mob Psycho 100 II
Best Drama Vinland Saga

Top Winner 2020:

Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba

Fun Fact:

Unusual Hosts:The live event was hosted by WWE professional
wrestler Xavier Woods (Austin Creed) alongside host Tim Lyu.


2021

The 5th Annual Crunchyroll Anime Awards (held in 2021 to honor the best of 2020 anime)
was full of historic sweeps, double wins, and unforgettable trivia.

Award name winner Image
Anime of the Year Jujutsu Kaisen
Best Animation Keep Your Hands Off Eizouken
Anime Trending Award Winner Kaguya-sama: Love Is War?

Top Winner 2021:

Jujutsu Kaisen

Fun Fact:

The hip-hop/funk band ALI achieved a rare feat by winning both Best Opening Sequence
(for "Wild Side" in Beastars) and Best Ending Sequence (for "Lost in Paradise"
in Jujutsu Kaisen) in the exact same year.


2022

The 2022 Crunchyroll Anime Awards featured major sweeps,
historic wins, and surprising contrasts between massive blockbusters and underdog favorites.

Award name winner Image
Anime of the Year Attack on Titan Final Season Part 1
Best Animation Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba Mugen Train Arc
Best film Demon Slayer – Kimetsu no Yaiba – The Movie: Mugen Train

Top Winner 2022

Attack on titan

Fun Fact:

Jujutsu Kaisen Domination: Entries targeted toward the shōnen demographic dominated
the 6th Crunchyroll Anime Awards, with Jujutsu Kaisen (Cour 2) taking home six individual awards

for the event with several historic milestones and surprising sweeps

Award name winner Image
Anime of the Year Cyberpunk: Edgerunners
Top Nominees for Anime of the Year Spy × Family
Ranking of Kings
Cyberpunk: Edgerunners

First in-person show since 2020: Due to the COVID-19 pandemic, both the

2024

Jujutsu Kaisen Season 2 dominated the 8th 8th Crunchyroll Anime Awards by sweeping a
massive 11 awards, including the coveted Anime of the Year.

Award name winner Image
Anime of the Year Jujutsu Kaisen Season 2
Best Animation Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba Swordsmith Village Arc
Best Anime Film Suzume

Top Winner 2024

Jujutsu Kaisen Season 2

Fun Fact:

New Categories Added: The 2024 awards introduced three brand-new categories to recognize
wider artistic excellence:Best Art Direction, Best Cinematography, and Best Slice of Life.


2025

The 9th annual Crunchyroll Anime Awards in 2025 featured a shift to May in Tokyo and broke records with 51 million global votes.

Award name winner Image
Anime of the Year Solo Leveling
Film of the Year Look Back
Best Continuing Series & Animation Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba

Top Winner 2025

Solo Leveling

Fun Fact:

New Categories Added: Crunchyroll expanded its lineup to 32 categories in 2025,
introducing Best Background Art, Best Isekai, and Best Hindi Voice Artist.


2026

The 10th annual Crunchyroll Anime Awards, held on May 23, 2026, in Tokyo,
Japan, was a historic milestone for the global anime community.

Award name winner Image
Anime of the Year My Hero Academia Final Season
Film of the Year Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba Infinity Castle
Best New Series Gachiakuta

Top Winner 2026

My Hero Academia Final Season

Fun Fact:

73 Million Voices: The 2026 edition saw a massive global engagement explosion,
securing a record-breaking 73 million fan votes cast worldwide to determine the winners.
